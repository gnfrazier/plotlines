Flow 1 — Trip initiation

A trip begins by deciding where to look. Plotlines opens on the shipped Buncombe County home region so the map is never blank, then asks for one location — which only centers the map — before the Author draws the bounding box that bounds layers, clusters, tiles, and elevation for the whole trip. Curation unlocks as soon as layers finish; elevation enriches behind the Author, and routing stays visibly disabled with its reason until it lands.
