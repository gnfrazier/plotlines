Flow 11 — Alternates: accommodation and branch

An alternate is a second path on a passage, and it is one of two things. An accommodation alternate is the same day at a different effort — a bypass or an extension a Character may take on their own copy. A branch is a story choice: the path carries its own anchors, its own narration, and its own reveal, so taking it changes what the day contains. The Author names which one they are making before they draw it, and the interface never blurs the two afterwards.
