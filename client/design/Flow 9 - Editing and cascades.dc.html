Flow 9 — Editing and cascades

Two mechanisms, never merged. Removing something the Author wrote states its scope and asks; invalidating something Plotlines computed marks it stale and says nothing. Tidying a mis-click costs no dialog, six edits in a row interrupt zero times, and the escalation only arrives at the point where a stale artifact would actually be believed — export, and then print.
