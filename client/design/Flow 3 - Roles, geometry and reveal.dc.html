Flow 3 — Roles, geometry and reveal

An anchor is one place with one arrival and as many roles as it needs. Each role carries its own kind, its own geometry — a point, an offset from the anchor, or an area — and its own reveal policy, so the restrooms can be visible from download while the story waits for arrival. Hazards and technical cruxes sit outside all of it: the model refuses to hide one.

01 The anchor and its roles Roles are a set, not a type. Add as many as the place actually plays.

04 Trying to hide a hazard Refused by the model, not by a missing button. Every path lands here.
Plotlines — Reveal policy
A hazard cannot be withheld.
Reveal does not apply to hazards or technical cruxes — not by role, not by day type, not by trip. It is a rule in the data model, so no setting anywhere can switch it off. If the surprise matters more to you than the warning, change the route rather than the hazard.
