Flow 7 — Account and session

Planning on the desktop works with no account and no network. Signing in is optional and adds one thing: sync. The only credential is a magic link, which is also the only recovery path, so the failure cases around it have to be honest. Sync checks the version on open and again on save, and never merges by itself.
