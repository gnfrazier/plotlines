Flow 5 — Multimodal day composition

A day is a sequence of passages, each with its own traversal mode — including driving, which is routed like anything else. Transitions between modes are authored objects with their own instructions. Day boundaries are set by distance and by the shape of the story, so a day can close on a resolution rather than a number, and hazards and cruxes are marked as they are found.
