Flow 4 — Explore and compose

Two ways to plan a day, switchable in both directions without losing work. In explore, distance and bands are constraints and the engine searches within them; when they conflict it names the conflict and offers relaxations. In compose, the promoted anchors are the spine and distance is a number the Author is told — an editing decision, never a solver failure. Both produce routes that are legal and physically passable in their mode.
