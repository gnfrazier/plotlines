Flow 10 — Foundational usability

Four things that cut across every other flow: authoring actions are reversible within the session and say so, every object type has a named path back to it, an empty view states a next action rather than an absence, and the desktop meets WCAG 2.2 AA as a floor. Two exclusions are deliberate and are stated where they apply — Author-note deletion is irreversible, and derived work is re-solved rather than undone.

The formal audit is a gate, not a leg
It sits before Plotlines expands past the Author desktop, because every added surface multiplies the cost of fixing the same problem — and desktop authoring is where none of the last three items has an owner today.
Deliberately out of scope for MVP: keyboard shortcuts and a command palette, a first-run tour, responsive layout past the desktop size classes, and cross-session undo. Decisions, not omissions.
