Flow 2 — Curation and promotion

Candidates are drawn from the live layers with their salience visible, and the Author can promote straight from the map — every stage after that is assistance, never a wizard. Co-location analysis runs only when asked, returns ranked proposals with the features that produced them, and remembers what was rejected. Promotion is the editorial moment: a place becomes an anchor with a role set, and the workspace keeps a third view for anchors that are not attached to a passage yet.
